contract: Full-Time Role

about: >-
  Premier Electrical Group is one of Northern Ireland's leading renewable energy
  specialists, providing high quality solar PV, battery storage and EV charging
  solutions for domestic, commercial and agricultural customers.


  Our mission is to help homes and businesses reduce their energy costs whilst accelerating the transition to renewable energy. We are committed to delivering exceptional workmanship, outstanding customer service and a seamless experience from initial enquiry through to installation and aftercare.


  As our business continues to grow, we are looking for passionate, hardworking individuals who take pride in delivering quality work, putting customers first and contributing to a team that values professionalism, safety and continuous improvement.
overview: We are seeking a qualified Electrician to join our expanding
  installation team, specialising in solar PV systems. You will play a key role
  in the installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance of electrical
  systems across domestic, commercial and agricultural projects. Working
  alongside roofers and other installation team members, you will ensure every
  installation is completed safely, efficiently and in line with current
  regulations, maintaining the high standards of quality and customer service
  that Premier Electrical Group is known for.
responsibilities:
  - Install, wire and connect solar PV systems.
  - Carry out testing, inspection and commissioning of installations.
  - Install inverters, battery storage systems and associated electrical
    equipment.
  - Diagnose and resolve electrical faults when required.
  - Ensure all work complies with current regulations and company procedures.
  - Complete all relevant certification and documentation.
  - Work collaboratively with roofers and the wider installation team.
  - Maintain high standards of health and safety at all times.
  - Represent Premier Electrical Group professionally on customer sites.
essential:
  - Fully qualified Electrician (NVQ Level 3 or equivalent).
  - Full UK driving licence.
  - Competent using electrical testing equipment.
  - Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  - Good communication skills.
  - Strong attention to detail.
desirable:
  - Experience with solar PV installations.
  - Testing and Inspection qualification (2391 or equivalent).
  - ECS or CSCS card.
  - Experience installing battery storage systems.
  - Experience working on domestic, commercial or agricultural projects.
benefits:
  - Competitive salary based on experience.
  - Pension scheme.
  - Healthcare plan.
