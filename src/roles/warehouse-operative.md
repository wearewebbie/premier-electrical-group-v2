title: Warehouse Operative
location: Ballynahinch, Co. Down
contract: Full-Time Role
published: true
about: Premier Electrical Group is a growing renewable energy company
  specialising in high-quality solar PV installations for residential,
  commercial and agricultural customers across Northern Ireland and the UK. We
  are committed to delivering reliable solar solutions while maintaining
  excellent standards of workmanship and customer service.
overview: >-
  We are looking for an organised and dependable Warehouse Storeperson to manage
  the day-to-day running of our solar equipment warehouse.


  The role involves managing stock, overseeing goods in and goods out, ordering materials and preparing complete kits for our solar installation teams. You will work closely with the operations and installation teams to ensure they have the correct materials and equipment for every job.
responsibilities:
  - Manage all goods entering and leaving the warehouse.
  - Check deliveries against purchase orders.
  - Report any missing, incorrect or damaged items.
  - Organise and safely store solar panels, inverters, batteries, mounting
    equipment and electrical materials.
  - Prepare complete material kits for upcoming solar installations.
  - Monitor stock levels and order additional stock when required.
  - Manage unused, returned or damaged materials.
  - Track tools and equipment issued to employees.
  - Keep the warehouse clean, safe, secure and well organised.
  - Follow all company health and safety procedures.
essential:
  - Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  - Experience managing stock and processing goods in and goods out.
  - Ability to maintain accurate stock records.
  - Comfortable using computers and stock-management systems.
  - Ability to work independently and manage daily responsibilities.
  - Good communication and teamwork skills.
  - Physically capable of safely handling and moving warehouse materials.
desirable:
  - Previous experience ordering stock and working with suppliers.
  - Experience within the solar, electrical, construction or renewable energy
    industry.
  - Forklift licence or previous forklift experience.
benefits:
  - Excellent rates of pay, depending on experience.
  - Company pension.
  - Private Healthcare plan.
